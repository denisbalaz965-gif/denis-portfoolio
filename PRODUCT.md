## Platform

web

## Users

Potential employers and people evaluating a work collaboration with Denis Baláž. They need to understand what he can contribute, examine concrete work and contact him about a suitable role.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio presenting Denis's AI-assisted web and digital product work alongside preparation and implementation of grant projects in the CASSIS Consult team. It should make his contribution, working method and the evidenced state of each project understandable. A relevant employment conversation is the intended outcome; no conversion result is claimed.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects practical digital work with experience handling project evidence, budgets, documents, dependencies and implementation. AI supports design, text, implementation and checking; Denis's decisions and responsibilities remain distinct from AI assistance and from the contributions of other team members.

## Product Principles

1. Show concrete work and explain Denis's own contribution.
2. Preserve the distinction between a claim, its evidence and the current project state.
3. Make AI assistance and team work explicit without substituting them for responsibility.
