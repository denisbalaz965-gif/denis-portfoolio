## Users

Businesses looking for their own web, e-shop, advertising campaign or automation. They need to understand the offer, inspect work by the people who will deliver it, and contact the team about a project.

A corporate-style portfolio under the approved studio name **d/m/m**, with no personal names or profiles in the public interface. The team behind the work remains Denis, Martin and Miroš as internal factual context. The owner changed the personal job-search portfolio into a three-person agency-style website on 9 November 2026. The visible offer is websites and applications, advertising and content, and automation/AI. Work is allocated to suit the project; no exclusive job titles or hierarchy have been established. The showcased web work is evidence of web delivery, not proof of PPC campaign performance or automation outcomes.

## Product Principles

1. Present work under d/m/m without personal names; preserve internal authorship and distinguish prior team-member work from commissions of the studio.
2. Make the team's offer understandable before asking a visitor to explore.
3. Keep independent work distinct from projects delivered together.
4. Preserve readable content and contact access independently of canvas or hover.
5. Separate supplied claims, observed public features and measured outcomes.
